the nano family

Eleven apps for people who don't code. Talk to an AI, ask a document a question, find a lost file, keep a folder safe, play your music — on your own computer, in plain words, with nothing to install.

Python 3.9+ is the only requirement works offline no accounts · no telemetry 800+ automated tests

The engine room

For people who want to see the gears — how a small AI is built, trained and run.

⚙️ nanollama.c — the inference engine

~1,400 lines of dependency-free C: transformer, BPE tokenizer, hand-rolled thread pool, int8 quantization.

🧬 nanobrain — training from scratch

A Llama-2 transformer in raw PyTorch — 16.9M parameters, 35 hours of CPU — exported to the engine's format.

🏗 nanoforge · 🎯 nanorl — studio and alignment

Describe an architecture, train it, export it — then teach it to answer with SFT and DPO.

Start here

  1. Install Python if you don't have it — python.org/downloads (tick Add to PATH).
  2. Pick an app above, open its Releases page, download the ZIP.
  3. Unzip it anywhere. Double-click run.bat (Windows) or run ./run.sh (macOS / Linux).
  4. A browser window opens with the app. That is all there is.
